# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We want participation in DSH Vibecode Studio to be harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct about technical problems and generous with the people solving them.
- Give credit for work and ideas.
- Accept constructive criticism; focus on what improves the project.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, doxxing, sustained disruption, or any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and comments that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban participants for serious or repeated violations. To report an incident, contact the maintainers privately through GitHub. Reports will be handled with discretion.

This code is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1, available at the link above.
